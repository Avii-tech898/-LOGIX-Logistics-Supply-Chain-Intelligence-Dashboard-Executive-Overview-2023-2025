# Business KPIs

## Executive
Total Orders, Total Customers, Total Revenue, Total Logistics Cost, Revenue per Order, Logistics Cost per Order, Failed Shipments, Shipment Failure Rate, Total Returns, Return Rate, Total Refunds, On-Time Delivery Rate.

## Delivery
Delivered Shipments, Delayed Shipments, Failed Shipments, Average Delivery Time, SLA Performance, Distance, Shipping Cost, Shipping Cost per KM.

## Warehouse & Inventory
Inventory Value, Current Stock, Low Stock, Out of Stock, Stock Risk Rate, Warehouse Throughput.

## Customer
Unique Customers, Orders per Customer, Average Order Value, Customer Segment, Repeat Customer Rate, Customer Lifetime Value where the analytical definition supports it.

## Returns & Payments
Return Count, Return Rate, Refund Amount, Return Reason, Payment Method, Payment Status.
