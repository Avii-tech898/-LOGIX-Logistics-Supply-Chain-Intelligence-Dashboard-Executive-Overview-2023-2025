# Business Problem

## Problem Statement
A logistics organization needs a unified analytics system to monitor delivery performance, warehouse efficiency, inventory movement, customer behavior, operational costs, payments, and returns.

## Key Business Questions
- How many orders and shipments are being processed?
- What is the revenue generated over time?
- What is the logistics cost and cost per order?
- How frequently are shipments delayed or failed?
- Which warehouses have inventory risk?
- Which delivery types perform differently?
- What are the major return reasons?
- How are customers distributed across segments?
- How are payment methods and payment statuses distributed?
- What operational patterns can support predictive decisions?

## Stakeholders
CEO / Management, Operations Manager, Warehouse Manager, Inventory Manager, Fleet Manager, Finance Team, Customer Team, Data Analyst.

## Analytical Goal
Convert raw operational data into validated KPIs, trends, diagnostic analysis, business insights, and eventually predictive models.
